Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside of operating the app on your behalf.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access. Your expense records are associated with your account and are not accessible to other users. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We use Google Fonts to serve typography assets — this involves a request to Google's servers when you load the app, subject to Google's own privacy policy. No expense data is transmitted to any external service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
